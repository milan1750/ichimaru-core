Frequently Asked Questions

Why has the name changed to Ichimaru Udon?

As we’ve grown across the UK, so has the case for a name and identity that reflects the business we are today. Ichimaru Udon is a new name and identity built from the ground up — but it’s the same team, the same recipes and the same great food. Read the full story.

What does "Ichimaru" mean?

Ichimaru is written 一丸. Ichi (一) means one, or unity — the character of togetherness: one table, one team, all gathered as one. Maru (丸) means round, or perfection — the character of wholeness, harmony and completeness. The brush stroke in our logo embraces the two in one: the mark of craftsmanship, made by hand.

Has anything changed apart from the name?

No. Same restaurants, same team, same recipes and the same noodles made fresh on site every morning. Some dishes now have simpler names on the menu — for example Kake Udon is now just Classic, and Kitsune Udon is now Age Tofu — but the food itself is unchanged.

Do you have vegan options?

We do indeed! Over 30% of our menu is suitable for vegans as we have created a tasty vegan broth that can be swapped out across many of our dishes.

Do I have to book?

No need to book. The great news is that we accept walk-ins only so you can come down anytime.

Do you have a children's menu?

We don’t have a specific children’s menu, however, as all our dishes are made from scratch, please speak to your server who will be happy to speak to you about adapting dishes to suit your little one’s tastes.

Do you offer halal dishes?

We do offer a range of halal dishes and all our chicken dishes are halal.

Do you do take-away?

Yes, we do! Our packaging is widely recyclable while still keeping our food tasting fresh.

Do you offer student discount?

Not at the moment.

Do you have gluten free noodles?

We do not offer gluten free noodles YET, but watch this space!

Do you accept AMEX?

We do indeed.

Can I book for a large group?

Yes. For 10 guests or more we’ll reserve space and can put together a set menu for you — send us the details through our group dining form and our events team will reply within 4 hours.

How do I make a complaint or give feedback?

Please use our contact form — it goes straight to our team and we aim to reply within two working days. If you have a severe allergy or feel unwell, don’t wait for a reply: call the restaurant directly and ask to speak to a manager.
