At the heart of everything we do is consistency, quality, and genuinely good food.

As we've grown across the UK, so has the case for a name and identity that reflects the business we are today — and where we're headed next.

So we made one. A new name and identity, designed from the ground up for the business we've become: Ichimaru Udon.

The Name

Ichimaru 一丸

Two characters. One idea — everything and everyone, gathered as one.

一

Ichi

one. unity.

The character of togetherness — one table, one team, all gathered as one.

丸

Maru

round. perfection.

The character of wholeness — harmony, completeness, perfection.

The brush stroke embracing in one —
the mark of craftsmanship, made by hand.

Our Craft

Made by Hand, Every Day

We never use pre-made noodles. Each batch of udon is hand-made on-site every morning before service.

1

The Dough

We combine high-protein flour, salt and mineral water to a precise ratio, kneading until the dough is smooth and elastic. This takes time and care — no shortcuts.

2

The Rest

The dough rests and develops gluten, which gives our udon that signature thick, chewy texture that cannot be replicated with dried or machine-made alternatives.

3

The Pull

Noodles are cut and cooked fresh to order in our open kitchen — you can always watch your bowl being made from start to finish.
